Quick Reference

The 3 Questions of Threat Modeling

  1. What am I protecting? — Identify the asset (data, identity, access).
  2. Who am I protecting it from? — Identify the adversary and their resources.
  3. What happens if I fail? — Assess the consequence (embarrassment, financial, legal, physical).
