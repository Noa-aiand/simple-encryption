How It Works

The PGP Practice Challenge

  1. Pick a practice partner below and copy their public key.
  2. Type a message in the Encrypt box and encrypt it using their key.
  3. Send the encrypted block to them with the Send button.
  4. They will reply with an encrypted message — decrypt it with your private key to read it!

Prerequisite: You must be logged in and have saved a public key in My Profile so the bot can encrypt its reply to you.
