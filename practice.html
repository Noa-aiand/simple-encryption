The PGP Practice Challenge

  1. Your goal: send me an encrypted message and prove you understand PGP.
  2. Copy my practice public key from the box below.
  3. Go to the Encrypt / Decrypt page and encrypt a message using my public key.

  5. I will decrypt it, read what you wrote, and encrypt my reply back to you (if you saved your public key in your profile).
  6. WARNING: If you send plaintext instead of a PGP-encrypted message, you will FAIL the challenge!
