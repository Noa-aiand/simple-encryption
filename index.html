What Does PGP Do?

PGP (Pretty Good Privacy) uses public-key cryptography: you share a public key so others can encrypt messages to you, and keep a private key secret to decrypt them. It gives you confidentiality, authentication, and integrity in one tool.

Get Started in 4 Steps

  • Generate a key pair on the Encrypt / Decrypt page.
  • Share your public key in My Profile.
  • Encrypt a message using the recipient's public key.
  • Decrypt received messages with your private key.

Practice Safely

This is a learning environment. Your keys are stored server-side for practice convenience — never use this app for real secrets. Once you are comfortable, move to a real tool like GnuPG or ProtonMail and keep your private key on your own device.
